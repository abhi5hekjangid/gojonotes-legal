Delete your Gojo Notes account

In the app: open Settings → Profile & plan → Delete account. You can download a backup first. Your account is disabled immediately, and your notes and account record are normally removed within about 20 minutes.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email support.gojonotes@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Gojo Notes account". We will verify the request and delete your account and notes.

What is deleted: your account, notes, folders, recall schedules and uploaded files. Limited pseudonymous usage counters used to prevent abuse of free limits are kept until the end of their week or month (about 31 days at most). Copies in providers' backups and logs are removed on their schedules.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
